What counts as a detour

Yes when both are true: the page does not tell the reader about the prerequisite until the point it is needed (or later, or never), and the page gives no steps for it, so the reader must stop and work it out or go to another page.

No when the page warns about it before it is needed, or gives the steps for it on the page.

Not a prerequisite when the reader does not need to have or set it up for this job. For example, it is the job's own main resource, or it does not apply.

The quoted text is where the extraction placed the need. It is a pointer and may be wrong.
