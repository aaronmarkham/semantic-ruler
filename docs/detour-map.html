The Detour Map

219 how-to pages, walked

You came to do one thing.

We had a model read 219 AWS how-to pages and list everything each one expects you to have first. Then we followed the links. Every page is a trail. Some run straight. Some fork by surprise. Some forks end at a wall.

04 · The odds at a fork

What is at the end of the link

Every time a page said "the steps are over there", we opened the page it pointed to and asked whether the steps for that one thing were on it.

05 · How sure

Three kinds of doubt

  • Which pages we happened to pick. That is the band on every bar above. Narrow a selection and the band widens; below five services we leave the band off.
  • Whether the model's reading is right. Still open. A person is labelling 60 of these by hand, blind to the model's answers. Until that score is in, treat every number here as a model's reading of the docs.

Pages are AWS documentation as fetched in November 2026. "Detour" means the page first mentions a prerequisite at the step that needs it, or later, or never, and gives no steps for it. "Dead end" means the linked page passes you on again, lacks steps for that thing, or is gone. The longer account, including the embedding ruler this project started from and what fell through, is in the companion page Rulers and Detours.
